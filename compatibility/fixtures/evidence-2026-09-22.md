screenshots themselves are **not committed** (this repo is public; full-window
shots are direct evidence of a specific licensed-app install on a personal

`%TEMP%\laelaps-studio-verify\`. Companion machine-readable evidence: the two
sanitized `TranslationMemoryUpgrade-*.log` files in this directory. In those
logs every local path is normalized to `<TESTDIR>` (= the per-user `%TEMP%`

so treat the committed copies as sanitized extracts, not raw artifacts.

## special_chars_display.png → §4

Translation Memories view, tab `special_chars [en-US->zh-CN]`, 2 rows:

| # | SOURCE | TARGET |
|---|--------|--------|
| 1 | `The spec says R&D output &lt; 5% error, use < and >.` | `规格。` |
| 2 | `Sales grew, e.g. in Q3 <they> doubled & tripled.` | `销售额增长了，例如第三季度<翻倍>并且&三倍。X` |

Row 2 TARGET carries a trailing `X` — the pending (uncommitted) test edit;
the commit attempt then failed with §3. Key point for the `esc()` check:
`&lt;` renders **literally** as `&lt;` (not decoded to `<`, not double-
escaped to `&amp;lt;`), and `<` `>` `&` display intact in both directions.
Left TM tree at this moment: basic, large, large_compet_test, special_chars.
